# User Stories

# Student Stories

## Story 1
As a student,
I want to browse subjects,
so that I can find learning materials.

---

## Story 2
As a student,
I want to watch embedded lesson videos,
so that I can learn directly on the platform.

---

## Story 3
As a student,
I want to download study notes,
so that I can study offline.

---

## Story 4
As a student,
I want to explore different tutors,
so that I can find a teaching style that suits me.

---

# Tutor Stories

## Story 5
As a tutor,
I want to create a profile,
so that students can learn about me.

---

## Story 6
As a tutor,
I want to upload lesson videos,
so that students can access my teaching content.

---

## Story 7
As a tutor,
I want to upload study materials,
so that students can download supporting resources.

---

## Story 8
As a tutor,
I want my lessons categorized by subject and syllabus,
so that students can find them easily.
