# Project Vision Document

## Project Name
EduBridge
---

# 1. Introduction

## Background
Many university students and private tutors struggle to find students to teach. At the same time, students struggle to find tutors that match their preferred teaching style and syllabus.

This problem is especially visible in Sri Lanka for:
- London syllabus
- Local A/L
- Local O/L
- Grade 1–11 education

---

# 2. Proposed Solution

The proposed platform is a free educational content and tutor discovery platform where tutors can:

- Share educational YouTube videos
- Upload study materials
- Organize lessons by subject and syllabus

Students can:
- Watch lessons for free
- Download materials
- Explore multiple teaching styles from different tutors

Unlike traditional course platforms, one subject can contain lessons from multiple tutors.

---

# 3. Main Objectives

## Student Side
- Easy access to free learning resources
- Ability to compare teaching styles
- Organized learning paths

## Tutor Side
- Gain visibility and promotion
- Build reputation
- Reach potential students

---
